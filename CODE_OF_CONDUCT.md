# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to a respectful, professional, and welcoming environment for everyone participating in EcfDgi.Client_C_Plus_Plus. The project covers C++20 software, the Drogon REST API and native SDK, electronic invoicing, XMLDSig digital signatures, and Dominican Republic DGII e-CF requirements. Technical disagreement is welcome when expressed constructively.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful, professional, and considerate.
- Critique technical decisions and evidence, never individuals.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience.
- Explain disagreements clearly and remain open to review.
- Respect project processes and maintainer decisions.
- Protect credentials, certificates, private keys, taxpayer information, and non-public vulnerability details.
- Use reproducible evidence when discussing fiscal behavior, XML, or cryptography.

## Unacceptable Behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing confidential information, deliberately misrepresenting test or compliance evidence, and repeatedly disrupting technical discussions are not acceptable.

## Technical Discussions
Disagreements about DGII specifications, XSD schemas, fiscal calculations, XML signatures, currency handling, or C++ architecture must remain evidence-based. Report suspected compliance issues with the applicable rule or official reference, a minimal synthetic example, and reproducible steps. Never publish real taxpayer or customer information.

A technical disagreement alone is not a Code of Conduct violation. Contributors may request clarification or propose alternatives through normal review channels.

## Maintainer Responsibilities
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports should be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against good-faith reporters is prohibited.

## Reporting
Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers using a private contact or reporting mechanism listed by the repository. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. For vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Include a description, relevant project space and approximate date, links or context, and any immediate confidentiality concern.

## Enforcement
Maintainers will review reports and determine a proportionate response. Actions may include a warning, content removal, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion.

## Scope
This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, project communication channels, and public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Attribution
This policy is informed by the principles and structure of Contributor Covenant 2.1 and adapted for this project's technical and security-sensitive context.
